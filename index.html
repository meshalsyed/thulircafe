Our Story

More Than a Cafe

Thulir began with a simple idea — that a good cup should feel like a small pause in a busy day. From the first pour of tea to the last sip of a cold mojito, every drink at Thulir is made to slow you down for a moment and bring a little warmth back into your day.

Whether you're stopping by for a quick coffee, a refreshing fresh juice, or settling in with friends over snacks and mojitos, Thulir is built to feel like your neighbourhood's own little escape — relaxed, welcoming and always fresh.
